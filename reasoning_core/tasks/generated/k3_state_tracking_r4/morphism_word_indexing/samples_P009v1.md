## Level 0

### Example 1

Prompt:

```
A uniform morphism over the alphabet {b, x} replaces each letter by a fixed string of length 2:
  b -> xx
  x -> xx
Beginning with the single-letter word x, applying the morphism 3 times produces a word of length 8.
What symbol is at position k = 11 (binary, 0-indexed) of that word?
The answer is the single lowercase letter at that position.
```

Answer: x
