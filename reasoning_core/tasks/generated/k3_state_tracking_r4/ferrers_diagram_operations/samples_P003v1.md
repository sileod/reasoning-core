# Level 0

## Example 1

A Ferrers diagram of a partition is a left-justified Young diagram whose rows contain the parts as row lengths, from top to bottom. The partition here has row lengths 5, 4, 1. A standard Young tableau (SYT) of shape given by this partition fills its cells with the numbers 1 through 10 exactly once, so that entries increase along each row and each column. The hook-length formula counts the number of SYT of a shape as n! / product of hook lengths. How many standard Young tableaux of this shape are there? The answer is one non-negative integer.

Answer:

288

## Example 2

A Ferrers diagram of a partition is a left-justified Young diagram whose rows contain the parts as row lengths, from top to bottom. The partition here has row lengths 4, 3, 3. The hook length of a cell is the number of cells to its right in its row plus the number of cells below it in its column plus one. Give the hook length of the cell in row 2 (1-indexed from the top), column 1 (1-indexed from the left). The answer is one positive integer.

Answer:

4
