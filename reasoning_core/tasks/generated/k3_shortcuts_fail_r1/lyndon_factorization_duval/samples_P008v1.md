### Level 0

Prompt:
A Lyndon word is a word that is strictly smaller (in lexicographic order, where 'a'<'b'<... ) than every non-trivial rotation of itself. Every word has a unique factorization into a sequence of Lyndon words that is non-increasing (factor i >= factor i+1 lexicographically); this is called the Chen-Fox-Lyndon factorization and Duval's three-way scan computes it in linear time. Given the word 'aaaaaaacc', give this factorization as the list of its factors in order, separated by single spaces.
The answer is a sequence of words separated by spaces (a factor that contains a space appears in double quotes).

Answer:
aaaaaaacc
