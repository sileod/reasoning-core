# Level 0

## Example 1

**Prompt:**

Over the complex numbers, consider the polynomial system in x:
  x**3 - 5*x**2 - 16*x + 80 = 0
Let I be the ideal generated by these polynomials; its common zero set is a finite collection of points in C^n.

Consider the product of two factors
  q = f * g,
  f = (x - 4),
  g = (x - 5).

Decide whether q vanishes on EVERY common complex solution of the system, i.e. whether q belongs to the radical of I (use the Rabinowitsch trick with a Groebner basis).

Answer Yes if it vanishes on every common solution, and No otherwise. State your conclusion.

**Answer:**

No
