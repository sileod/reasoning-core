### Level 0


Prompt:
A lattice is made of sites holding integer charges and a set of interaction terms. A pairwise potential between two sites contributes coupling*(c_a*c_b); a multisite potential over a set of sites contributes coupling*(product of their charges). The lattice energy is the sum of every interaction term.
Sites: site 0 charge -2, site 1 charge 2, site 2 charge 1, site 3 charge -2.
Interaction terms:
P0: pairwise potential between sites 1 and 3, coupling -1
P1: pairwise potential between sites 2 and 3, coupling 1/4
P2: pairwise potential between sites 0 and 1, coupling -1
M0: multisite potential over sites 0,1,2, coupling -1
M1: multisite potential over sites 0,2,3, coupling -4
Apply these edits simultaneously to the initial state:
change coupling of interaction M1 to 1
change site 0 charge to 1
Give the total change in lattice energy after all edits, i.e. final energy minus initial energy, counting a term affected by more than one edit only once. Express the result as a reduced fraction with numerator and denominator separated by a slash (denominator positive), e.g. '7/3' or '-5/2'. If the result is an integer, still write it as n/1.

Answer:
2/1
