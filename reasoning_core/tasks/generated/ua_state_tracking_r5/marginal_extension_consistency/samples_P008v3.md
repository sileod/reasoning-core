## Level 0

### Example

**Prompt:**

Three variables A, B and C each take one value from {0, 1}.

P(A,B): rows are A values, columns are B values.
0: 4/13 3/13
1: 7/26 5/26

P(B,C): rows are B values, columns are C values.
0: 4/13 7/26
1: 7/26 2/13

A joint distribution over (A,B,C) consistent with both tables above is known to exist (the tables are compatible). Across every such joint distribution, what range of values can the probability P(A=0, C=1) take? State the minimum and the maximum as two reduced fractions separated by a space, e.g. '1/4 3/8'. Give your answer.

**Answer:**

0 11/26
