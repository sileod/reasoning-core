Prompt:

A finite outcome distribution P assigns positive integer weights [2, 2, 1] respectively to the distinct integer outcomes [0, 2, 3] (weight sum 5). Its mean is 7/5. Let U be the uniform benchmark over the same 3 outcomes, giving each outcome probability 1/3. The minimal integrated tail difference between P and U is the 1-Wasserstein distance W1(P, U), equal to the sum over every adjacent gap of (gap length) times the absolute difference between the cumulative probabilities of P and U across that gap. Compute W1(P, U). The answer is one reduced fraction in the form p/q.
