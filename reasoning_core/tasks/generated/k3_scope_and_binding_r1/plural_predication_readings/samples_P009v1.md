# Level 0

## Example 1

**Prompt:**

Consider a model with 3 atoms, and evaluate the plural predication over every sum of two or more of these atoms.
The loud atoms are: a1, a2.
Distributive reading holds under a sum iff every atom in the sum is loud; collective reading holds iff at least one atom in the sum is loud.
The sums under consideration are: a1a2, a0a2, a0a1, a0a1a2.
For each sum give the truth verdict triple (distributive, collective, cumulative) as 0/1 for false/true.
Cumulative is true iff distributive or collective is true.
Answer with one semicolon-separated triple per sum, in the order the sums were listed. For example 1,1,1;0,1,1 means the first sum is true on all three readings and the second is only collectively and cumulatively true.

**Answer:**

1,1,1;0,1,1;0,1,1;0,1,1
