# Level 0

Prompt:
You hold lots of one stock. For each lot you know the day bought, units bought, cost per unit, day sold, and sale price per unit. Lots are ordered by buy day (ties by their listed order).
Lots:
  lot 0: bought day 1, 5 units @ 14, sold day 4 @ 8 per unit
  lot 1: bought day 2, 10 units @ 6, sold day 5 @ 1 per unit
  lot 2: bought day 4, 4 units @ 17, sold day 8 @ 16 per unit
  lot 3: bought day 8, 2 units @ 11, sold day 8 @ 5 per unit
Wash-sale rule: a sale is a wash sale (loss disallowed) if within 30 days after the sale you buy any replacement lot of the same stock, bought strictly after the sale day. The disallowed loss is added to that replacement lot's basis (the earliest-buy-day replacement wins if several). A loss is recognized only if the sale is not a wash sale.
What is the adjusted basis (cost basis including any transported wash-sale losses) of lot 2? Answer with a single integer.

Answer: 68
