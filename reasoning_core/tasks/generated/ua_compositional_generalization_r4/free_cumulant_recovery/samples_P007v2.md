# Samples for P007v2 (free_cumulant_recovery)

## Level 0

### Prompt

Let X be a free random variable with free cumulants:
k_1 = 1, k_2 = -1.
Its moments follow the moment-cumulant relation, where moment m_n is the sum over noncrossing partitions of 2 points of the product of cumulants indexed by block size.
The computed moments are:
m_1 = 1, m_2 = ???.
What is the integer value of the missing moment m_2? Answer with the integer only.

Answer:

`0`

## Level 2

### Prompt

Two freely independent random variables A and B have free cumulants:
kA_1 = -3, kA_2 = 4
kB_1 = 3, kB_2 = 5
.
An ordered mixed moment is the sum over noncrossing partitions of the positions of the word of the product over blocks of the cumulant of the block's sole symbol, indexed by block size; any block that mixes A and B contributes zero.
The following mixed moments are known:
m[A,A] = 13, m[B,A] = -9, m[A,B] = -9, m[B,B] = ???.
What is the integer value of the missing moment m[B,B]? Answer with the integer only.

Answer:

`14`

## Level 5

### Prompt

.
An ordered mixed moment is the sum over noncrossing partitions of the positions of the word of the product over blocks of the cumulant of the block's sole symbol, indexed by block size; any block that mixes A and B contributes zero.

m[B,B,B,B] = 31279, m[A,B,B,B] = -12445, m[A,A] = ???, m[B,A,B,B] = -12445.
