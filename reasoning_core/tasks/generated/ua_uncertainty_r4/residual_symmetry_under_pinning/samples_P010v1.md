# Level 0

Prompt:

Consider the dihedral group D_7 of symmetries of a regular 7-gon whose vertices are labeled 0,1,...,6 in cyclic order; its elements are the rotations and the reflections of the polygon. In the group, the marked vertices [0, 2] are fixed pointwise, so we keep only the symmetries that map each one to itself. Let H be the subgroup of D_7 consisting of exactly those symmetries, and consider the action of H on the vertex set {0,...,6}. Report the orbit partition of this action as a list of blocks, each block a sorted list of vertex labels, and the blocks sorted by their smallest element. For example, if the orbits are {0}, {1,3} and {2}, write [[0], [1, 3], [2]].

Answer: [[0], [1], [2], [3], [4], [5], [6]]
