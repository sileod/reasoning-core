## Level 0

**Prompt:**

A two-branch quantum instrument acts on the qubit state |0> = (1,0)^T. Branch 1 applies the Kraus operator A = [[3, -2], [-2, 1]] and branch 2 applies B = [[2, 0], [0, 2]]. The outcome of the measurement is forgotten, so after the instrument we hold the incoherent mixture rho = (A |0><0| A^T + B |0><0| B^T) / p, where p is the normalization (trace) and A^T is the transpose (all coefficients are real). Report the normalized conditional state rho as its three independent real entries [rho00, rho01, rho11] where rho01 = rho10, as three space-separated fractions (for example '1/2 0 1/2' or '1 0 0'). Give only those three fractions.

**Answer:**

13/17 -6/17 4/17
