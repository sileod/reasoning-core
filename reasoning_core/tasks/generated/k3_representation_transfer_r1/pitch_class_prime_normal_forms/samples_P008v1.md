## Level 0
### Example 1
**Prompt:** Given the pitch-class set [3, 6, 8] (pitch-class integers 0-11), compute its prime form: reduce to normal order, then compare under transposition, inversion, and rotation, selecting the most compact ascending form that starts on 0. Return the prime form as a list of ascending integers, e.g. [0, 3, 7]. Answer for [3, 6, 8]:
**Answer:** [0, 2, 5]
