# Samples for support_function_composition (P009v1)

## Level 0

### Example 1

**Prompt:**

A convex shape is the Minkowski sum of the convex hulls of the point sets P=[(-7, -18), (-5, -15), (2, 8), (3, 11), (12, 0), (17, 3)] and Q=[(-14, 6), (-13, 15), (-4, -2), (4, 13), (6, -15), (14, 2)] (Minkowski sum is the set of all p+q with p in the first hull and q in the second). The support function value in direction d=(-1, 2) of a shape S equals max over S of the dot product with d. The support function of a Minkowski sum is the sum of the individual support functions. What is the support value of the Minkowski sum of the two hulls in direction d=(-1, 2)? Answer with one integer.

**Answer:**

62
