## Level 0

### Example 1

An attentional system receives the following target series in order: T1, T2*, T3*, T4*, T5, T6. Items marked * are attended; unmarked items are distractors and are ignored. Whenever an attended target is admitted, the gate enters a blink of the stated recovery period and cannot admit any further target until it recovers; the blink decrements by 1 per item while in effect. Here the consolidation recovery period is 1 item(s). Which attended targets are actually admitted, in presentation order? Answer as a comma-separated list like 'T2,T5', or 'none' if none.

Answer: T2,T4
