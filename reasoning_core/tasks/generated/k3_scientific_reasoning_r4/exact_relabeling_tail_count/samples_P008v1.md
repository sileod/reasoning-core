**Prompt:**

Rank the 4 distinct values from 1 (smallest) to 4 (largest), and take as the test statistic the sum of the ranks of the group-A values. The observed group-A rank sum is 7. An exact permutation test relabels the groups in every possible way, i.e. for every choice of which 2 of the 4 values lie in group A. A relabeling is at least as extreme as the observed one when its group-A rank sum deviates from the expected rank sum 5 at least as much as the observed one does, i.e. |rank-sum - 5| >= |7 - 5|. The observed assignment itself counts. Count how many such arrangements there are and answer with that single integer.

**Answer:** 2
