# Samples for focus_particle_equivalence (P003v1)

## Level 0

#### Example 1

**Prompt:**

    Situation model: a set of people {Bo, Ida, Ed, Cy, Flo} and the property 'attended the workshop'.
    Each version below asserts something about who has the property. For focus-particle and cleft constructions, work out, for the focused constituent, which alternatives the form EXCLUDES (forces to lack the property) and which it forces to have the property. Two versions are truth-conditionally identical exactly when they force the same excluded alternatives and the same present alternatives.
    1. The ones who did not attend the workshop are Ida
    2. Even Ida attended the workshop
    3. It is Ida who attended the workshop
    4. The ones who attended the workshop are Ida
    5. Only Ida did not attend the workshop
    Version 1 is the reference. List, in increasing order and separated by commas (always including version 1 itself), every version whose truth conditions are identical to version 1's.

**Answer:** 1,5
