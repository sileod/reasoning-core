## Level 0
### Example
**Prompt:**

A coalition has party weight capacities [4, 3, 2] (the weight of party i may be any nonnegative integer up to its capacity).
A party is winning iff its chosen weight is strictly greater than the quota 1.
The required labels are: party 0 is winning; party 1 is winning; party 2 is losing.
Find the least possible sum of all party weights realizing exactly these labels, or 'none' if impossible. Answer as 'min:<integer>' or 'none'.

**Answer:**

min:4
