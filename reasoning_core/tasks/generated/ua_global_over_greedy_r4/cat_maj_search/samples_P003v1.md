Level 0

Prompt:
Two probability distributions over 2 outcomes are w = 5/6 1/6 and x = 1/6 5/6 (entries are fractions of a common denominator). For a probability vector y whose entries are positive multiples of 1/4 summing to 1, write the tensor w⊗y for the length 2·d vector of all pairwise products w_i * y_j. For two same-length vectors u and v, u is majorized by v, written u ≺ v, when, with u and v each sorted non-increasing, every leading partial sum of u is at most the corresponding leading partial sum of v and the totals are equal (Hardy-Littlewood-Pólya). A vector y is a catalyst for the relation w⊗y ≺ x⊗y when that relation holds, and the dimension of y is the number of its entries d, which ranges from 1 to 4. No coordinate of y is fixed. Find the minimum dimension d of a catalyst y, meaning the least d for which there exists such a y with w⊗y ≺ x⊗y, where the entries of y are a_1/4, a_2/4, ..., a_d/4 with positive integers a_i summing to 4. Answer with the minimum integer d, or the single word none if no dimension d works.

Answer: 1
