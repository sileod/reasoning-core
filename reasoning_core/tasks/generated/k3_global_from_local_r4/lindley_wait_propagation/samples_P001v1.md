## Level 0

**Prompt:**
A single server processes jobs that arrive at integer times. Arrivals (in time units between consecutive jobs) are [1, 2, 1, 1] and service times are [1, 5, 6, 3]. The wait of a job is how long it waits for service after arriving, computed by the Lindley recursion w_{n} = max(0, w_{n-1} - arrival_n + service_n). What is the wait of job index 2 (0-based)? Answer as an integer if it is whole, otherwise as a fraction num/den in lowest terms.

**Answer:**
8
