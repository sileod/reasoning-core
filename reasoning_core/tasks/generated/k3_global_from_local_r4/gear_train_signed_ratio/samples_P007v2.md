## Level 0
### Example 1
Prompt:
A gear train drives an output shaft from a driver. The driver, shaft 0, is turned clockwise at speed 1.
The connections of the train are:
- shaft 0 connects to shaft 1 with a crossed belt (pulleys 6 : 16)
- shaft 1 and shaft 2 are mounted on one common shaft
- shaft 2 connects to shaft 4 with an open belt (pulleys 17 : 18)
- shaft 1 connects to shaft 3 with a crossed belt (pulleys 12 : 9)
- shaft 3 meshes with shaft 4 (teeth 4 : 18)
- shaft 4 and shaft 5 are mounted on one common shaft
Rules: a gear mesh transmits the ratio (teeth on the upstream shaft)/(teeth on the downstream shaft) and reverses direction; an open belt transmits the upstream/downstream pulley radii keeping direction; a crossed belt reverses direction; two gears on one common shaft share the same speed and direction (1:1).
The output is shaft 5. Give its signed speed as a reduced signed ratio like '3/2' or '-5/4', counting clockwise as positive. If the topology makes two routes from the driver to the output give clashing signs or ratios, answer with the single word 'jam'.
Answer:
jam
