# Samples for tiered_price_lot_clipping (P009v1)

## Level 0

A firm buys an item under a stepped unit price: quantities 0..18 cost $32/unit; quantities 18..any amount at or above cost $24/unit. All units in one lot are charged that tier's unit price. Annual demand is 2933 units, each order costs $84 to place, and holding cost is 7 times the unit price paid, per unit per year. Minimizing total annual cost = ordering cost (84*2933/q) + holding cost ((7*unit price*q)/2) + material cost (q*unit price), find the integer lot size q that achieves the minimum. Report 'q|cost' where q is that integer and cost is the minimal total annual cost rounded to the nearest integer.

Answer: 48|10316.75
