## Level 0

### Example 1
**Prompt:**

There are 4 claims for a divisible resource with a supply of 14 units. Claim 4 wants 2 units at the most and no claim may receive more than it asks. For fairness you raise every unfulfilled claim toward one common level until some claim reaches its cap or the supply runs out, then freeze the saturated claims and re-level the rest, stopping when the whole supply is used or every claim is capped. The claims are, in order: [9, 2, 12, 2]. What allocation does every claim receive? Give the allocation as a list of integers, one per claim in the same order, e.g. [1, 2, 3].

**Answer:**

[5, 2, 5, 2]
