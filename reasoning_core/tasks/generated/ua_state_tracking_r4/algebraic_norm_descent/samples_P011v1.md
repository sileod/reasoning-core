# Level 0

## Example 1

Consider the tower Q=K_0 < K_1=Q(t_1) of number fields, where each intermediate field K_i is generated over K_{i-1} by adjoining t_i subject to the prime-degree defining polynomial: t_{1}^{2} = q.
Each t_i is an algebraic integer, and the whole tower sits in the top field Q(a)=Q(t_1,...,t_d), where a=t_d satisfies the Eisenstein polynomial a^{2}=13 (13 is a rational prime), giving step degrees 2. Because every step is prime, the field norm N_{Q(a)/Q} descends one prime degree at a time through the intermediate fields K_{d-1}, ..., K_1.
Let alpha = -2 + (-1)*t_{1}, written as a rational combination of the tower generators.
Compute the ground-field norm N_{Q(a)/Q}(alpha). By transitivity of the norm along the tower it is a rational integer.
Give your answer as exactly that integer (no other text).

Answer: -9
