## Level 0
Prompt:
We place weights on the cells of a cyclic (toroidal) grid of 3 rows and 3 columns, each entry an integer in [0,5). The two-point displacement correlation of a displacement (dr,dc) is the sum over all cells g of w(g)*w(g+(dr,dc)), indices wrapping around. Treat (dr,dc) and (-dr,-dc) as the same displacement.
Grid weights:
0 1 4
3 1 0
3 4 4

Are all these two-point displacement correlations equal, i.e. do they take one common value for every nonzero displacement, unchanged under every translation and reflection of the grid? State your verdict in one word, either the affirmative or the negative, and nothing else.

Answer:
no
