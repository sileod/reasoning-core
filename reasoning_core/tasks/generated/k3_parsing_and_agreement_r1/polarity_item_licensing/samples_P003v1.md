## Level 0
**Prompt:**
Licensing rules: a polarity item is licensed by exactly one of these, or unlicensed.
- negation: 'not' licenses an item in its own clause.
- only: 'Only X' licenses an item later in the same clause.
- few: 'Few N' (downward entailing) licenses an item in the same clause.
- question: a question licenses an item in its own clause.
- conditional: an 'if'-antecedent licenses an item in that antecedent.
- comparative: a 'more ... than' construction licenses an item in the than-clause.
- adversative: 'denied/doubted/regretted' license an item in their 'that'-complement.
A negation, only, few or question outside the item's own clause does not license it.
In the text below, the polarity item is 'any' and it appears exactly once.
