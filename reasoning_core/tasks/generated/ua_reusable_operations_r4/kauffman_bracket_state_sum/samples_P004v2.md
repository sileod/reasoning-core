A tangle diagram has 6 crossings and resolves (when all its loops are formed after smoothing) into disjoint simple closed curves, of which 1 arise from a particular smoothing. The Kauffman bracket local smoothing rule assigns to each A-type resolution a factor of -A^(-3) and to each B-type resolution a factor of -A^(3); each closed loop contributes a factor of (-A^2 - A^(-2)). Consider the single smoothing state in which exactly 3 of the 6 crossings are resolved A-type and 3 are resolved B-type, and this state produces 1 closed loops. Write the contribution of this one smoothing state, simplified, as a monomial in A (with integer coefficient, using A^0 -> 1 and A^1 -> A). Answer with the monomial only.

Answer:
-A^2
