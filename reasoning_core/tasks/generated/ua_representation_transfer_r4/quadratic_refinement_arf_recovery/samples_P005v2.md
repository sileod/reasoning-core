# Level 0

### Example 1

**Prompt:** Over GF(2) the quadratic refinement Q on the symplectic space V of dimension 4, with the standard mod-two intersection pairing, is Q(x) = x2 + x3 + x4 + x1x2 + x1x3 + x2x4 (mod 2). The Gauss sum is G = sum over x in V of (-1)^Q(x), equal to plus 2^(n/2) when the Arf invariant is 0 and to minus 2^(n/2) when it is 1. The sum runs over all 16 vectors of V. State the Arf invariant as a single bit.

**Answer:** 0
