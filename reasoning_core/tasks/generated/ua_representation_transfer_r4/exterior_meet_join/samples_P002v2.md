# Samples P002v2 - exterior_meet_join

## Level 0

Prompt:
In the projective plane, a point is a 1-blade and a line is the exterior join of two points. The point P = (5, 3, -6) and the line L = a ∧ b with a = (0, 3, -2), b = (-2, -5, -1) are given. Their regressive meet (intersection subspace) is the point P itself when it lies on L, otherwise it is empty. Say whether the meet exists; if it does, give the meet point in canonical integer form (coordinates divided by gcd, first nonzero entry positive), e.g. (2, -3, 1); otherwise answer 'empty'.

Answer:
empty
