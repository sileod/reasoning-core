## Level 0
### Example 1
**Prompt:** Let f: C -> P^1 be a degree-4 branched cover of the Riemann sphere (base genus 0). At a branch point whose ramification partition splits the preimage into m distinct sheets, the local branching contribution is d - m. Riemann-Hurwitz states 2g - 2 = -2d + sum of (d - number of sheets) over all branch points, where g is the genus of C. Consider a cover of degree 4 with target genus 0 and the following ramification partitions over its 3 branch points, where each list sums to 4 and the number of entries is the number of sheets: P1: [?], P2: [2, 2], P3: [4]. Branch point P1 is marked [?] with an unknown positive integer number of sheets. The unique integer that makes the total branching balance Riemann-Hurwitz at genus 0 is the number of sheets of P1. Answer with a single integer: that number of sheets.
**Answer:** 3
