# Level 0

## Example 1

An urn is sampled without replacement, one ball at a time, and the run stopped because a white ball appeared, on draw 1.
Two candidate hypotheses are being compared:
H_1: the urn holds 3 white and 2 black balls (5 total).
H_2: the urn holds 4 white and 1 black balls (5 total).
Compute the log2 likelihood ratio of the observed stopping report, log2( P(report | H_1) / P(report | H_2) ), summing over every draw history consistent with the report. Round to the nearest integer. Give only that integer.

Answer: 0

## Example 2

An urn is sampled with replacement, one ball at a time, and the run stopped after 2 successive black balls, never having seen a white ball.
Two candidate hypotheses are being compared:
H_1: the urn holds 3 white and 2 black balls (5 total).
H_2: the urn holds 2 white and 3 black balls (5 total).
Compute the log2 likelihood ratio of the observed stopping report, log2( P(report | H_1) / P(report | H_2) ), summing over every draw history consistent with the report. Round to the nearest integer. Give only that integer.

Answer: -1
