## Level 0

### Example i=0

Prompt:

A gadget wires some of the parts below into a chain: the output of one part feeds the input of the next, internal nodes are hidden, and the external relation is the relational composition of the chosen parts (each part at most once).

Part 0: {(1,3), (2,2)}
Part 1: {(1,1), (1,3)}
Part 2: {(2,2)}

Target external relation: {(1,1), (1,3)}

Can such a gadget be built with exactly this external relation? Answer 'possible' or 'impossible'. If possible, minimise the number of parts used.

Answer:

possible
