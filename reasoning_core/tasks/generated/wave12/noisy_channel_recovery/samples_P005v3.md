## Level 0

### Example 1

**Prompt:**

A noisy channel sent a sequence of color names. Each intended color was independently left unchanged with probability 0.7, or replaced by one of its synonyms with probability 0.3. The received sequence is: lime, olive. Recover the set of distinct intended colors, and write them as a comma-separated list sorted alphabetically (e.g. 'blue, green, red').

**Answer:**

green
