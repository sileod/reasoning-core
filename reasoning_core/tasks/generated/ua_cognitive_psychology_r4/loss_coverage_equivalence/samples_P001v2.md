# samples_P001v2

## Level 0

### Example 1

**Prompt:**

Below are two insurance policy descriptions, Policy A and Policy B. Their clauses are written in a different order and possibly reworded.

Policy A:
The yearly limit refreshes and is reset after every claim.
Payouts for kitchen appliances are reduced by a 25 dollar deductible and limited to 281 dollars per year.
Payouts for electronic equipment are reduced by a 8 dollar deductible and limited to 351 dollars per year.
Combined annual payouts are capped at 552 dollars.
The insured bears a 23 dollar deductible on every occurrence.

Policy B:
Each occurrence receives a fresh annual limit once the previous one is spent.
Each loss occurrence is reduced by a 23 dollar deductible.
Combined annual payouts are capped at 552 dollars.
electronic equipment carries a per-item deductible of 8 dollars and an annual cap of 351 dollars.
Payouts for kitchen appliances are reduced by a 25 dollar deductible and limited to 281 dollars per year.

Do Policy A and Policy B produce identical payouts on every possible loss history, accounting for per-item and per-occurrence deductibles, per-item caps, aggregate caps, exclusions and limit reinstatement? Answer with exactly 'same' or 'different'.

**Answer:**

same
