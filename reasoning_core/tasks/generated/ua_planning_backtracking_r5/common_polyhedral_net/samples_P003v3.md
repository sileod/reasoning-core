## Level 0

**Prompt:** Label the 6 faces of every box: 0 top, 1 bottom, 2 front, 3 back, 4 left, 5 right. A planar net of a box is its 6 faces unfolded flat into a single connected, non-overlapping piece by keeping some edges joined (hinged seams) and cutting the rest. You have several boxes; each lists face pairs that must stay joined in the net: 'must-join seams' (edges forbidden to cut) and 'face-correspondence pairs' (face pairs that must be directly joined as a seam). Decide whether a single common hinged-edge set is a valid planar net for every box while including all of its required joints.
Box A: 1x2x1 cuboid. Must-join seams (forbidden to cut): none. Face-correspondence pairs (must be directly joined): none.
Box B: 2x2x1 cuboid. Must-join seams (forbidden to cut): none. Face-correspondence pairs (must be directly joined): none.
Does one common net exist that is a valid planar net for every box and includes every required joint? Answer 'yes' if such a shared net exists and 'no' if it does not.

**Answer:** yes
