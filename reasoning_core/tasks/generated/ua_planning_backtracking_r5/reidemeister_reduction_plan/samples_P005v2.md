## Level 0
### Example 1
**Prompt**
A knot diagram is encoded as a cyclic list of labeled strands, where 'Xb' is the inverse (reverse crossing) of 'X'. A Reidemeister-I reduction collapses an adjacent backtrack pair 'X Xb'. A Reidemeister-II reduction collapses two equal strands 'X ... X' provided the strands strictly between them share no label with the rest of the diagram. Starting from the diagram [Ab, Bb, B, A, A, A], can it be reduced to the empty diagram using at most 4 such reduction moves in total? Respond with a single word.
**Answer**
yes
