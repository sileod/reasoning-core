## Level 0

### Prompt

A vessel's cross-section is a basin whose bottom is a staircase valley through the points (0,4) (3,5) (12,5) (x increasing left to right), with a vertical side wall rising from the leftmost point to height 7 and from the rightmost point to height 7; the two rim tops are joined straight across the open top at height 7. Its full width is 12 (left rim at x=0). A removable internal wall stands at x=3, so before it is removed the liquid is confined to the basin portion at x <= 3.

The vessel starts empty. In order, apply exactly these operations, one after another:
tilt the whole cross-section by -12 degrees about the center of its top rim, 
add 11 units of liquid

After every operation the liquid settles with a horizontal surface and is held by the vessel only up to the level of its lower rim; any liquid that would rise above that rim overflows and is discarded, and it cannot be recovered later. What integer amount of liquid is retained in the vessel after all operations are applied? The answer is one integer.

### Answer

7
