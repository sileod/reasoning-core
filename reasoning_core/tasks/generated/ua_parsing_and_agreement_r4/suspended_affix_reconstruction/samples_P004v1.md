# Samples: suspended_affix_reconstruction

## Level 0

### Example

A coordinated phrase suspends a shared affix: a prefix or suffix that scopes over every conjunct is written once, and the conjunct stems are linked by hyphens. Recover the full expanded form of each conjunct: its own affix concatenated directly onto the shared stem, with no hyphen. List them in order, separated by ' | '. Phrase: micro- and super-fire

**Answer**: `microfire | superfire`
