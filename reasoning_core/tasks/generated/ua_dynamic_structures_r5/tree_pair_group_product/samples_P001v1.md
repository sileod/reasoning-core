## Level 0
### Example 1
Prompt:
Every element below is a product of paired binary trees: each pair's left and right tree share a common middle subtree, and their leaf labels form a reduced word over the generators a, b. An upper-case letter is the inverse of the matching lower-case generator (A cancels a, B cancels b, C cancels c).
Tree pair 1 shares the middle subtree and carries the expansions Abb (left) and ABA (right).
Multiply the elements by refining the shared middle subtrees, carrying the expansions outward, and cancelling matching carets (adjacent generator then its inverse). Give the freely reduced product as one string with no spaces; if it reduces to nothing answer `e`.
Answer:
Abbaba
