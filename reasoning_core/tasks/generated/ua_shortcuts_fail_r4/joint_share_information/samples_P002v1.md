# Samples P002v1 - joint_share_information

## Level 0

Prompt:

A secret bit is produced by a deterministic encoder from a biased random bit R and two correlated share bits S0,S1. R equals 1 with probability 0.180 and 0 otherwise, independent of everything else. The share bits are correlated with a hidden uniform bit H: S0 equals H with probability 0.50 and S1 equals H with probability 0.50. The encoder truth table gives the secret as a function of (H,R): g(0,0)=0, g(0,1)=1, g(1,0)=1, g(1,1)=0. The unconditional probability that the secret is 1 is 0.500.
The coalition observing both shares {S0,S1} either recovers the secret, has perfect secrecy, or suffers partial leakage. Answer the outcome.
State only your final answer.

Answer:

perfect

## Level 2

Prompt:

A secret bit is produced by a deterministic encoder from a biased random bit R and two correlated share bits S0,S1. R equals 1 with probability 0.180 and 0 otherwise, independent of everything else. The share bits are correlated with a hidden uniform bit H: S0 equals H with probability 1.00 and S1 equals H with probability 0.68. The encoder truth table gives the secret as a function of (H,R): g(0,0)=0, g(0,1)=0, g(1,0)=1, g(1,1)=1. The unconditional probability that the secret is 1 is 0.500.
Which nonempty subset of {S0,S1} is minimal among those that fully determine the secret from what they observe? Answer '0' (only S0), '1' (only S1), '01' (both), or 'none'.
State only your final answer.

Answer:

0
