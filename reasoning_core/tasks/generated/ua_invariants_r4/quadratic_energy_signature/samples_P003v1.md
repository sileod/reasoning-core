# P003v1 samples: quadratic_energy_signature

## Level 0

### Example 1

**Prompt:**

A quadratic energy is E(x) = x^T A x where A is the symmetric matrix (one row per line):
24 36
36 54
Under every invertible real change of coordinates the number of positive, negative and null directions is invariant (Sylvester's law of inertia). Give the invariant signature as three integers p,n,z separated by commas, e.g. '1,2,0' means one positive, two negative and no null directions. The answer is exactly 'p,n,z'.

**Answer:**

1,0,1
