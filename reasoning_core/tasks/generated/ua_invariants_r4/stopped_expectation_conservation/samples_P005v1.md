## Level 0
**Prompt:**
Consider a finite Markov chain on the integer states 0,1,...,3. The states 0 and 3 are absorbing; the states 1..2 are transient. A particle starts at state 2. At each step, from transient state i it moves to i-1 with probability 1/2 and to i+1 with probability 1/2. Define an observable h that is harmonic on every transient state: for each transient i, h(i) equals the expectation of h at the next state reached from i. The boundary values are h(0) = -2 and h(3) = -1. The process is stopped at tau = min(first time the particle hits {0, 3}, 2), i.e. after at most 2 steps or on hitting an absorbing state, whichever is first. By harmonicity h forms a martingale, so the expected terminal value E[h(X_tau)] equals the harmonic value h(2) at the start state, which is uniquely determined by the boundary values and the transition structure. Compute h(2).
Give the answer as a reduced fraction p/q or an integer, with no other text.

**Answer:**
-4/3
