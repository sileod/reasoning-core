## Level 0

**Prompt:**
Over a finite linear order, consider ordered free variables x0 < x1 < x2 < x3. Eliminate the quantified variable y from the statement 'there exists y such that y - x2 = 4 and x3 - y = 1', where y lies strictly between x2 and x3. Give an equivalent condition on the free variables as a conjunction of atomic gap constraints using only '<' between a pair and exact gaps 'u - v = k' (e.g. 'x0 < x1 & x2 - x1 = 5 & x2 < x3').

**Answer:**
x0 < x1 & x1 < x2 & x3 - x2 = 5
