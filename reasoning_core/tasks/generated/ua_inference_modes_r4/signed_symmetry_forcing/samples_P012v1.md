## Level 0

Prompt:

Two arrays A and B are indexed by pairs of letters. The entries obey these signed index-symmetry relations (each "X = s\u00b7Y" means the entry X equals s times the entry Y, so a step with s=-1 flips the sign):

1. A(a,b) = -1·A(c,c)
2. A(c,c) = -1·B(b,a)
3. B(b,a) = 1·A(b,a)
4. A(a,b) = -1·A(a,a)
5. A(a,a) = 1·A(a,c)
6. A(a,c) = 1·A(b,a)
7. A(a,b) = 1·B(a,c)

Determine the relation between the symmetric components A(a,b) and A(b,a). Combine the relations until A(a,b) is expressed in terms of A(b,a): if the composition forces A(a,b) to equal both A(b,a) and its negation, then both entries must vanish. Reply with one token only: a plus sign, a minus sign, or the word zero, corresponding respectively to a relative factor equal to positive one, negative one, or a forced-vanishing pair of entries.

Answer: 0
