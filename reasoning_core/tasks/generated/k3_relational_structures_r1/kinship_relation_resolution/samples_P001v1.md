# Level 0
## Example 1
Prompt:
In this family each line records that the second person is the parent of the first person:
P0 is the parent of P1.
P1 is the parent of P2.
P0 is the parent of P3.
P0 is the parent of P4.
P0 is the parent of P5.

Find the lowest common ancestor of P4 and P2 (the deepest ancestor they share), then state their relationship as a cousin designation with removal, e.g. "first cousins once removed" or "second cousins". Answer exactly that designation.
Answer: first cousins once removed
