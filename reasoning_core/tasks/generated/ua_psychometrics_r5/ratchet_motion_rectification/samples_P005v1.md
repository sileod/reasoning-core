# Level 0

An escapement converts alternating strokes into retained motion. Strokes are [-2, 1, -2]. The pawl direction is backward; only a stroke whose scaled value v = stroke * direction falls inside the engagement window [-2,2] is retained, and the rest slip without producing motion. Tooth pitch is 1. What is the net advance retained? The answer is one value.

Answer: -3
